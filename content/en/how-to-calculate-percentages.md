Percentages show up in discounts, tips, taxes, interest and exam results. Once you know four simple formulas, you can handle almost all of them.

## 1. Find a percentage of a number

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

**Result = number × percentage ÷ 100**

Example: 15% of 240 is 240 × 15 ÷ 100 = 36. A quick shortcut: 10% of a number is the number divided by 10, and 5% is half of that.

## 2. Apply a discount or an increase

To reduce a price by a percentage, subtract that percentage of the price. To increase it, add it.

- 20% off 80: 80 × 20 ÷ 100 = 16, so the new price is 80 − 16 = 64.
- You can also multiply directly: 80 × 0.80 = 64.
- A 10% increase on 64 is 64 × 1.10 = 70.40.

Notice that 20% off followed by 10% on top does not bring you back to 80. Percentages always apply to the number in front of them at that moment.

## 3. Calculate percentage change

To find how much a value changed in percentage terms:

**Change % = (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100**

Example: a price goes from 80 to 92. The change is (92 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 15%. A negative result means a decrease.

## 4. Find the original value

If you know the price after a discount, divide by the remaining fraction. A jacket costs 64 after a 20% discount, so the original price is 64 ÷ 0.80 = 80.

The same idea works for tax. A price of 120 that includes 20% VAT has a net price of 120 ÷ 1.20 = 100 and a tax amount of 20. Notice that the tax is not 20% of 120.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Going up and down by the same percentage.** A 50% increase turns 100 into 150. A 50% decrease from there gives 75, not 100.
- **Percent versus percentage points.** If an interest rate rises from 4% to 5%, it increased by 1 percentage point, but by 25% in relative terms.
- **Subtracting tax the wrong way.** To remove a tax rate from a gross price, divide by (1 + rate), not multiply by (1 − rate).

## Quick tips for tipping

To tip 15%, work out 10% (move the decimal one place) and add half of it. On a bill of 64, 10% is 6.40, half of that is 3.20, so the tip is 9.60.
