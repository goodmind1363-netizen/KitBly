A meta description is the short summary that often appears under your page title in search results. It does not directly improve your ranking, but it can decide whether someone clicks. Here is how to write one that works.

## Is there an official length limit?

No. Search engines do not publish a fixed character limit. They show a snippet whose length depends on the width of the screen, and they sometimes ignore your description and write a different one from the page content.

In practice, descriptions are cut off when they get too long, so a commonly cited target is roughly **120 to 160 characters**. Shorter text is safer on mobile screens, where less of the snippet is shown. Put the most important information at the start in case the end is trimmed.

## Title tag versus meta description

- **Title:** the clickable headline. Aim for about 50 to 60 characters so it is not truncated.
- **Description:** the supporting summary, about 120 to 160 characters.

Both should be different on every page of your site.

## How to write a good meta description

1. **Say what the page offers.** Describe the benefit for the reader in plain language.
2. **Include the main topic naturally.** Words in the search query are often highlighted, which helps your snippet stand out.
3. **Add a reason to click.** A concrete detail works better than a vague promise, for example "with a worked example" or "free and runs in your browser".
4. **Write for people.** Avoid stuffing keywords or repeating the title.
5. **Make every description unique.** Duplicate descriptions give searchers no way to tell your pages apart.

## Example

Weak: "Loan calculator. Calculate loan. Best loan calculator online."

Better: "Estimate your monthly payment and total interest for a fixed-rate loan. Enter the amount, rate and term for an instant result."

The second version is clear, specific and about 130 characters long.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the description empty and hoping the search engine will pick something useful.
- Copying the first sentence of the page without checking it makes sense on its own.
- Using the same text across many pages.
- Making claims the page does not support. This leads to quick exits.

## How to check the length

Count the characters, including spaces, and keep an eye on the result. Our word counter shows the character total as you type, so you can adjust your text before you publish it.

Remember that a good description supports the page but cannot rescue a weak one. Start with useful content, then summarise it honestly.
